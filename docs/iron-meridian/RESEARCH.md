## Iron Meridian translation

The useful design lesson is the coupling between decisions: construction creates capacity, capacity produces equipment, equipment and manpower train divisions, logistics limits those divisions, research changes the available equipment, and diplomacy changes where armies can operate. This is an inference from the relationships above and a design direction for Iron Meridian.

Iron Meridian's names, countries, map, visual treatment, rules, balance, and code are original. Its compact browser simulation must be assessed on its own behavior. A functioning connected subset can provide a playable strategy game, but it does not establish native Hearts of Iron IV accuracy or content parity.

Major unproven parity areas include native formulas and tick ordering; province and state history; complete equipment and division designers; doctrines and commanders; operational planning; local rail, hub, naval and air logistics; full air and naval mission resolution; country-specific focus/event/decision content; occupation and resistance; peace conferences and subjects; intelligence operations; facilities, scientists and special projects; every DLC interaction; save compatibility; multiplayer synchronization; accessibility and native interface behavior; performance at full global scale.
