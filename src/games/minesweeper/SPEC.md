# Minesweeper specification

A quiet expedition on a molded mint field. Reveal every safe tile; a mine ends the run. A number counts mines among the up to eight neighboring cells. A zero opens its connected zero component and numbered boundary. Flags block reveal and flood but never count toward winning. A revealed number chords only when the neighboring flag count equals its number. Incorrect matched flags can lose; the chord resolves a hidden mine before safe targets.

Beginner is 9 × 9 / 10 mines; intermediate 16 × 16 / 40; expert 30 × 16 / 99. A new field has a uint32 seed but no mines. First reveal samples the exact mine count without replacement outside the click and all its neighbors. Every supported field has enough space for that protection. Partial Fisher–Yates uses seeded Mulberry32 and rejection-bounded integers. Given random uint32 draws, each allowed mine subset has probability 1 / C(eligible cells, mine count); the PRNG is for reproducibility, not cryptography. Flagging before the first reveal does not alter sampling.

Flood traversal is iterative BFS over in-bounds eight-neighbor coordinates; reveal on enqueue prevents duplicate expansion and recursion overflow. The clock begins on reveal, ends on win/loss, and excludes paused time. Score is ten per safe tile plus twenty per mine on winning. Parent toolbar supplies new game/restart, pause, rules, settings and persistence. Difficulty changes start a fresh field.

Mouse click/touch tap/Enter/Space reveals, right-click/480 ms hold/F/Flag mode toggles flags, and click on an open number/C chords. Movement over ten pixels, pointer cancel, pause, restart and disposal cancel a hold. A consumed pointer remains tracked until release; only its matching release click is suppressed, without a duration limit. Cancellation and lifecycle resets clear that gesture state. The board has semantic grid/row/gridcell/button structure, coordinate/state labels, one tab stop, arrows and row Home/End. Cell sizes 34/44/54 px allow larger targets; the 34 px default keeps beginner compact. Wide fields scroll within the board. Numbers, distinct mine/flag SVG silhouettes and raised/flat tiles supplement color. No animation is required.

Version-1 snapshots retain seed, first reveal, exact derived cells, revealed/flag masks, status, exploded index, elapsed time and optional cell size. Restore checks lengths/types/ranges/disjoint masks and terminal consistency, regenerates every mine/number from seed and first click, and ignores incompatible data. All event handlers use one AbortController, with owned clock/hold timers cleared on dispose.

Rules were checked against GNOME Mines' official help (https://raw.githubusercontent.com/GNOME/gnome-mines/master/help/C/rules.page); that source defines numbered adjacency and safe-cell completion. First-click neighborhood protection and the exact supported sizes are explicit implementation choices. Exact research and skill use are recorded separately in SKILL_USAGE.md.
