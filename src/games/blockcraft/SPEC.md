# Blockcraft — endless block building

A first-person sandbox in an endless procedural world of 16×16×64 chunks. Walk, sprint, jump and swim; mine the outlined block and place any of nine blocks against it. Creative-style: there is no inventory, health or crafting. The score is the number of blocks mined in this world (monotonic); placed blocks are counted too.

**Origin.** The game design follows the MIT-licensed [Minecraft-Javascript-Edition](https://github.com/ashish0kumar/Minecraft-Javascript-Edition) (commit `f7336ea`): chunked simplex terrain, sand below water level, coal and iron ore, trunk-and-canopy trees, top-layer clouds, a hotbar, mining with the left button, placing with the right, and saved edits. The implementation is a rewrite for speed; see [the optimisation record](../../../docs/BLOCKCRAFT.md). Its textures, font, pickaxe model and name were not used. All pixel art is painted in code (`atlas.ts`), and the cover is generated by `scripts/generate-blockcraft-cover.py`.

**World.** Seeded 2D simplex continents, hills and ridged mountains set column height (3–56). Sea level is 26: low columns are sand beaches and underwater floors, high ones bare stone and then snow. Interpolated 3D noise carves caves, which may open on land but never under water. Coal (below 54) and iron (below 34) form small clusters in stone; bedrock fills y = 0. Trees are placed by a column hash and generated from a two-column margin, so a canopy crossing a chunk border is identical on both sides. Generation is a pure function of seed and chunk position.

**Rules.** Bedrock and water cannot be mined; blocks cannot be placed inside the player or outside 0–63. Unloaded chunks are solid, so the player never falls into ungenerated space; falling below the world respawns at the spawn point. At most 60 000 edited voxels are kept per world; beyond that the status line explains the limit.

**Controls.** Desktop: Play captures the mouse (Pointer Lock). Without capture, drag to look and click to act. W A S D or arrows move, Space jumps (a tap shorter than a frame still jumps), Shift sprints, 1–9 and the wheel select, middle click picks the targeted block, holding a button repeats every 230 ms, and F3 shows performance. Touch: drag to look, left stick moves (pushed fully, it sprints), ▲ jumps, tap mines, long-press places, and the hotbar is tappable. View cycles 3, 5, 7 and 9 chunk view distances.

**Lifecycle.** Escape releases the mouse; the arcade then opens Study as usual. Pause, Study, dialogs and hidden pages stop the loop, drop the pointer lock and clear held input. Resume shows a Resume screen, since capture needs a gesture. Snapshots hold seed, position, look, slot, counters, view and the edit list. `restore` validates every field and the whole edit list before replacing anything. Restart makes a new seeded world. Disposal terminates the workers, frees every geometry, material and texture, and forces WebGL context loss.
