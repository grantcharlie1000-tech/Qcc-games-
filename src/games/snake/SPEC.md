# Snake — continuous authoritative candidate

This local candidate replaces the reviewed discrete checkpoint with continuous travel and an explicitly changed turn cutoff. Runtime `ffa5e45` has passed local verification and independent review; no publication or deployment occurred. Historical versions and evidence remain in `docs/evidence/snake-redesign/`; current evidence belongs in `continuous-authoritative/`.

- Garden: 16 × 16 grid centers. Initial head `(5,6)`, three earned segments, first fruit `(9,6)`, seeded reproducible food, ten points per fruit. Travel speed is one cell per 160 ms; there is no speed selector.
- A leg is a straight horizontal/vertical journey between grid centers. Its heading locks when it starts. Up to two legal turns queue for future centers, relative to the executing/last queued heading; duplicates and reversals are rejected. The initial direction input can select the first leg. Input after a junction cannot redirect the already-started leg, including a leg going toward a wall.
- The event clock advances to input delivery time **before** accepting a turn. Pause and serialization also flush elapsed active time. RAF, input and saves use the same monotonic performance clock; an older RAF timestamp cannot rewind its baseline. Start, resume, active v2 restore and legacy continuation establish a fresh event-time baseline, so offline/paused time is never caught up.
- Positions are authoritative continuous model coordinates. Rendering copies the same orthogonal head/body trail, retaining every bend; there is no predicted endpoint, previous-to-current projection, diagonal shortcut or added visual arrival offset.
- Shared solid shapes: head disk radius **0.40 cell**, body round tube/caps radius **0.40**, fruit-flesh disk radius **0.33**. Head contacts food at center distance 0.73; self contact uses 0.80. Decorative stem, leaf, highlight and shadow are not solids. The connected first **1.20 cells of body arc** are excluded from self collision, permitting normal right-angle bends without treating the attached neck as an obstacle.
- Walls occupy the board edges, half a cell beyond the outer grid centers. Head-wall contact therefore occurs at center coordinate `−0.10` or `15.10`, potentially before another junction. An outward leg from an outer grid center can end before its queued next turn.
- Collision/eating use analytic swept disk/capsule events, including moving tail and neck-trim endcaps. Intervals split at junctions, clipping corners and growth completion. Contact is processed even mid-frame/mid-leg; loss has priority over eating at a simultaneous contact. Tangency moving away is not an approaching collision. Numerical geometry tolerance is 1e−9 cell, with a separate 1e−11 ms junction-roundoff tolerance (genuine 1e−8 ms pre-junction windows remain uncommitted); tiny residual steps retain their clock/growth budget without creating an unresolvable polyline edge.
- Eating grants one earned segment and one cell of growth credit immediately. That credit holds the actual tail for the next cell of head travel, so visible growth is continuous. Tail entry is safe only when its swept solid shape has actually cleared; a growth-held tail may remain an obstacle. Food spawning enumerates grid centers that do not penetrate the body/fruit contact shapes and excludes head contact; the LCG advances once per spawn.
- Winning means **256 earned segments / 2530 points**. Victory freezes at the final fruit contact and may retain its final one-cell growth credit; it is not a claim that every pixel/cell is already covered by the drawn body. Invalid early-win saves are rejected.
- State version 2 stores continuous trail endpoints/bends, current leg origin/progress, executing heading, queue, earned segment count, growth budget, seeded food, active time/effect timestamps and phase. Pause/resume and v2 reload preserve the exact fractional pose, queue, growth and effect ages. Validation checks finite/ranged fields, coherent path length, orthogonal grid-junction bends, no zero edges/backtracking/crossings, head/heading/progress coherence, contact solids, queue legality and victory invariants.
- Version-1 saves preserve body coordinates, seed, food, score, phase, direction, queue and tick count. Malformed ready/running v1 headings or first saved queued turns pointing back through the neck are rejected; perpendicular headings and lost saves’ fatal attempted directions remain compatible. Their fractional accumulator represented waiting time, not a continuous pose: it is archived as `migration.elapsed` and movement restarts from the saved safe junction. Converted running saves remain held behind **Continue saved run**; shell pause/resume alone does not start them. First continuation consumes the first saved queued turn; a legal incoming direction can append behind that queue. Startup checks the prospective first executed heading against the actual neck before mutating ready/held state; unsafe input leaves the save unchanged for safe retry. A turn appended behind a valid first saved turn keeps that priority and executes at a later center. Version 2 records that continuation once, preventing repeated migration or time credit. Legacy optional effect ages are imported only when the original raw save is v1, then validated and preserved on the v2 timeline. Version 2 owns its nullable/active/expired timestamps even at time zero; retained migration markers and optional presenter metadata cannot reimport or override them.
- Core continuous travel is essential gameplay in both normal and reduced-motion modes. Live OS/app reduced motion suppresses only decorative food/eating effects. Original shaded body/eyes/fruit, a single subtle spawn opacity settle and short eating/facial feedback use no flashes, idle pulses or screen shake.
- The garden fits both available width and height; roomy outer/playable sizes are 728 / 694.4 CSS px (40% above original 520 / 496). Controls remain at least 48 px and can sit beside the board. Full-DPR backing follows CSS size and DPR-only changes while paused. Playfield corner radius is capped at half a cell so it cannot clip valid corner travel; the decorative rim does not overlay the playfield.
- Keyboard input ignores repeats/modifiers/editable controls/open dialogs; mouse/touch direction buttons and dominant-axis swipes share the same event-time action. Pause, pointer cancellation and capture loss discard partial swipes. Disposal aborts listeners, disconnects resize observation and cancels RAF. No shared shell/package/library/external-service changes are part of the candidate.
