# Quadfall

Arrange seven four-cell pieces in a ten-column well with two hidden rows. Complete horizontal rows to clear them. A shuffled seven-piece bag feeds the next queue; hold can be used once per piece. A piece locks after 500 ms on the floor, with at most 15 movement or rotation resets at the same lowest row; reaching a new lowest row resets the timer and count. Pieces spawn in the visible rows when space permits, falling back to the hidden rows when necessary. A blocked spawn or a piece locking entirely above the visible well ends the run.

A one, two, three or four-line clear scores 100, 300, 500 or 800 times the current level.

A T piece locks as a T-spin when its last successful action was a rotation (no later move or drop) and at least three of the four diagonal cells around its centre are blocked by blocks, walls or the floor. It is a full T-spin when both corners on its pointing side are blocked, or when the rotation used the fifth kick; otherwise it is a mini. Full T-spins score 400, 800, 1200 or 1600 for zero to three lines; minis score 100, 200 or 400 for zero to two. Quads and T-spins that clear lines are difficult clears: a difficult clear directly after another difficult clear (no ordinary line clear in between; locks without lines do not break the chain) is back-to-back and scores 1.5 times its base. Each consecutive locking piece that clears lines extends a combo; the second clear in a row adds 50, the third 100, and so on. Leaving the well empty is a perfect clear and adds 800, 1200, 1800 or 2000 for one to four lines. All of these are multiplied by the level in effect before the lines are counted. Each clear, its bonuses, level-ups and points are announced to screen readers. Soft drop adds one point per cell; hard drop adds two. Level is 1 plus completed lines divided by 10, rounded down. Gravity is `max(50, 1000 * 0.82 ** (level - 1))` milliseconds per row.

Left/right or A/D move; Down/S soft drops; Up/W/X and Z rotate; Space hard drops; C/Shift holds. When left and right are both held, the most recently pressed one moves the piece; releasing it hands movement back to the other. Touch controls offer the same actions; left, right and soft drop repeat while held. Narrow screens show two next pieces. Cleared rows animate for 320 ms without pausing play: their cells whiten and shrink from the centre outwards, then the rows above ease down into the gap, and the ghost is hidden until it finishes. With reduced motion the cleared rows only fade in place. Hard drops leave a short trail unless reduced motion is enabled. The ghost piece shows the landing cells in the piece's colour, and a grounded piece dims as its lock delay runs out. Drag horizontally or down on the board, tap to rotate, or flick up to hard drop. Escape opens Study through the host. The host stores the versioned run and local best; signed-in accounts sync both.

A restored running game waits for Continue; returning from Study resumes the current game without that prompt.
