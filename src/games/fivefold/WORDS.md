# Word sources

Answers are five-letter words from American/3esl.txt in [12dicts 6.0.2 by Alan Beale](https://wordlist.aspell.net/12dicts/), explicitly public domain, intersected with ENABLE. Guesses are five-letter words from [ENABLE enable1.txt](https://github.com/dolph/dictionary), public domain, plus the answers.

The LDNOOBW English list (CC BY 4.0) is used only at build time and is not shipped. It and a manual blocklist remove offensive, sensitive and crude words and US-only spellings such as color, favor and honor from answers; slurs are removed from guesses. The daily order uses Python `random.Random(20261027)` shuffle.
