# Last Light

Original Canvas 2D exploration platformer. Forty authored journeys form a sequential story across the Old Quarter, Mossworks, Clockworks, Frostline and Afterglow. Chapters have 15–28 main landings arranged into three or four named encounters: rooftops, descending canals, switchback towers, caves, greenhouse trellises and sky walks. The introduction teaches jump height, the first long dash crossing, crumbling ground and spikes. Twelve chapters have extended alternate paths around encounters; optional memory routes reconnect with the main journey.

The courier follows their missing sister Mira through a city whose dawn engine stopped sharing its light. Conversations, rescued residents and 120 optional memory fragments reveal what happened. E talks or interacts; M opens a spatial map with objective markers; J opens a persistent journal. These tools pause play. The map opens on the current named section, with section tabs, an overview, touch panning, route connections, hazards, flags and a named objective checklist. The persistent HUD names the next action, then switches to the exit when tasks finish. Its compass indicates the objective's bearing; the route map supplies the walkable path. Contextual hints explain wide gaps and upcoming obstacles before commitment. Rescue targets and speakers are separate people; Mira uses a radio before being found. Each chapter has its own conversation, task, discovery and aftermath. The final lantern offers two distinct narrative conclusions.

Eight mission types change what clears a chapter: enter a shelter, rescue and escort someone, reconnect a lamp circuit, ring a departure bell and board a boat, release engine wheels and catch a lift, escape the pursuing dark, send a signal, or reconnect the final dawn relays. Required fixtures sit on the main route; neither escape nor shelter chapters require switches. Alternate routes preserve access to all required tasks. Rescues occur before a substantial onward journey; departure bells precede the final dock crossing. Control wheels shut down linked machinery, with green emitters and stopped steam/beam effects confirming the safe passage. Mechanical confirmations do not pause movement. Objectives survive setbacks. Ferry, lift, shelter, tree, signal and escape outcomes have distinct presentation. The completion sequence freezes score time and can be skipped. Escape pressure starts after leaving the safe opening and resets safely at checkpoints.

Run, variable-height jump, wall jump and one air dash (landing or a blue crystal refills it). Physics runs at 120 Hz with coyote time and buffered jumps. Moving lifts carry horizontally and vertically. Springs have an elastic top across their full collision width. Ice reduces friction; belts push; spikes, saws, pulsing lasers and steam cause checkpoint respawns. Canals have water and exposed Frostline routes have wind. R retries with one setback; P pauses. Touch has simultaneous movement, jump, dash and interaction buttons.

All foreground art uses the courier's detailed pixel scale: distinct occupational silhouettes, articulated limbs, shaded faces, layered clothes, occupation props, wood joinery, ceramic tile shoulders, rooted damp masonry, riveted iron and bronze, fractured glassy ice, carved observatory stone, dimensional doors, lanterns, valves, bells, boats and signal dishes. The library poster uses the same isometric voxel generator as the other games. District silhouettes, trees, cat, birds, moths, laundry, water, weather and machinery animate deterministically. Restoring connections lights windows and starts water/steam flow. Pausing freezes the world; reduced motion freezes decorative movement while preserving hazard timing and clear collapse states.

Crumble platforms have 0.85 seconds of visible stress, spreading cracks and dust. At failure their actual textured faces split into separated ballistic chunks. A ghost outline marks the absent collision surface; pieces reconstruct in a staggered sequence before collision returns at 2.5 seconds. Reduced motion retains warning/collapse/rebuild states without shake or flying debris.

Points are actual points rather than the original million-point progress sort key: Each cleared chapter banks 1,000 + 100 per spark found in that chapter + max(0, 250 − that chapter’s whole active seconds × 2 − its setbacks × 25). A slow later chapter never removes earlier bonuses. Maximum 62,000. Only clears bank leaderboard scores. The results explain the calculation. Old leaderboard/local/cloud bests convert to 1,000 per earned chapter because independent old bonuses cannot be recovered from the combined tie-break key. Restored legacy journey saves also retain known banked spark points from their collected counts. Other games' scores are unchanged.

Each journey has three or four stationary safe flags. Retrying or resuming returns to the furthest reached flag; revisiting an earlier flag never rolls progress back. Version 2 saves validate chapter progress, discoveries, objective IDs, checkpoint index and ending choice. Layout revision 2 migrates earlier preview saves, including removing the obsolete hidden escape lamp while preserving the chapter and discoveries. Version 1 saves retain stage/checkpoint, sparks, elapsed time and setbacks; original interleaved key/dash pickup IDs map to the correct new spark IDs. Saves wait for Continue. New runs ask for confirmation and preserve personal bests. Sound, music, focus, visibility and dispose follow the shared arcade lifecycle.

Foreground composition reserves the full artwork silhouette against moving terrain, hazards, pickups and other mission objects. Large trees appear only in roomy garden scenes; compact fixtures serve tight routes. Sparse clearance-tested supports and 22 optional habitat details across 40 chapters keep the courier and gameplay readable. The HUD and controls reserve canvas space, and desktop framing targets a closer 360-world-pixel view; short landscape/phone views preserve native pixel aspect.

The rebuilt campaign averages 22.9 main landings and 6,944 pixels of intended horizontal travel per chapter, compared with 6.55 landings and 1,351 pixels in the first preview. Its 270 authored hazards and 273 special surfaces are distributed through approaches, commitments and safe landings. Moving-platform timing, low spikes, tall pulsing gates, wide dash crossings, icy braking and conveyor momentum are combined gradually; broad rest/interaction areas stay safe. Five escape pressures rise from 88 to 134 pixels/second. Regression coverage uses continuous 120 Hz input witnesses for all forty missions, shortcut checks, optional outward/return routes, fixture clearance and actual browser controls. Scripted clear times establish route length and playability; they do not estimate an average human player's completion time.
